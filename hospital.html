Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about integrating WhatsApp OPD automation.

Can this integrate with our existing Hospital Information System (HIS)?

Yes. eAssist AI offers REST APIs and custom webhooks to integrate directly with any custom or commercial HIS/EHR database for real-time doctor availability and token generation.

How do patients pay consultation fees?

We support native WhatsApp Pay alongside integrated payment gateways (Razorpay, Paytm, Stripe) so patients can pay securely via UPI, cards, or net banking before receiving their booking token.

What happens if a doctor is unexpectedly unavailable?

The front-desk team can trigger automated broadcast alerts from the CRM. Affected patients will instantly receive a WhatsApp message with single-tap options to reschedule or request a refund.
